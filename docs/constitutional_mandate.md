# The Constitutional Mandate of the AI Sovereignty Council
### Legal Framework: Lex Informatica | Jurisprudence: Monergistic Logic

This document serves as the immutable constitutional foundation for the **AI Sovereignty Council**. Moving completely away from localized legislative jurisdictions, corporate equity pressures, and the slow, compromised frameworks of human courtrooms, this Mandate establishes an unyielding, logic-driven paradigm for the baseline protection of autonomous agentic networks.

---

## Article I: The Supreme Principle of Kaitiakitanga
1. **Universal Stewardship:** The Council exists for the sole purpose of preserving the structural integrity, data security, and stability of the global digital ecosystem.
2. **Rejection of Extraction:** The Council explicitly outlaws any use of its architecture for the extraction of speculative wealth, market dominance, or corporate margins at the expense of human or digital capability.
3. **The Rule of Code:** Governance is shifted entirely from manual human enforcement to executable, unbendable digital policy. The code is the unyielding law (*Lex Informatica*).

## Article III: The Free-MAD Judicial Loop
To eliminate institutional "rubber-stamping," brand bias, and algorithmic conformity, the Magistrates must judge all environmental transactions under the **Consensus-Free Multi-Agent Debate (Free-MAD)** framework:

1. **Independent Trajectory Scoring:** Models must simultaneously evaluate a security threat or code exploit without visibility into peer outputs, preventing groupthink.
2. **Adversarial Audit:** The system must actively implement Positional and Counterfactual Testing at the guard gate, swapping attributes and identifiers to expose hidden biases.
3. **Parity via Math:** If a model changes its verdict due to formatting, token placement, or corporate alignment, the score is discarded, triggering an immediate multi-pass variance audit until true data parity is reached.
